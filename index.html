How the showcase works

An agent turns a fire report into a costed decision

Navigator watches the suppliers of Nordwerk Automotive, a fictional maker of electric cars. When a warning comes in, its AI agent checks it, works out what it costs and proposes a response. You decide.

This demo: a fire at a supplier in Tangier
The reportA fire at a wiring-harness plant in Tangier, not yet confirmed
Our exposureThe plant makes 55 % of Leipzig's main wiring harnesses
If nothing is doneLeipzig stops on day 9, with €19.1M at risk over 30 days
The agent's proposalOption B for €984k leaves €1.9M at risk

Five steps, four figures, one decision

The agent spots the report, checks it, lets an engine calculate the impact, proposes three costed options and prepares drafts. The four figures at the top say what that is worth.

The company is fictional, the arithmetic is real

Nordwerk, its suppliers, stock and prices are invented; the places and routes are real. In this replay the agent's words and sources are scripted, and an engine computes every euro and day.

Where everything comes from
ScriptedThe agent's words and its web sources in the replay, marked as representative
ComputedEvery euro and day, by a deterministic engine from Nordwerk's data; the AI only picks between costed options

Show it in five minutes

Five steps, a minute each; the bar on the right says what to press.

  1. Spot: it starts by itself; point at the four figures. “€19.1M at risk, and Leipzig stops in 9 days unless we act.” 1 min
  2. Check: press Run step 2. “One report is not enough: the agent finds a second source.” 1 min
  3. Calculate: press Run step 3. “An engine, not the AI, prices five weeks at €19.1M, not €935k.” 1 min
  4. Propose: press Run step 4, then Approve option B. “€984k cuts the risk to €1.9M; a person decides in about 4 min.” 1 min
  5. Prepare: it drafts by itself; press Approve all. “Four emails and one booking wait for approval. Live, this costs €0.20.” 1 min
